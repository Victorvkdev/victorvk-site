      Meet Vk.

      I'm Victor, but everyone calls me Vk. I started in game design, and it still shapes how I work: I look for the moment someone hesitates and ask what would make the next step feel obvious, maybe even fun.

      I led a team of four designers across 13+ products in education, health and construction. From 2025 to 2026 I owned the gamification of three regulated consumer brands, from research to code, and led the 4Play redesign that was later presented as a reference in Europe, Cyprus and Latin America.
