About me

Games taught me how to keep people playing

I'm Victor, but everyone calls me Vk. I've been hooked on technology since I was a kid, and I still play RPGs and video games whenever I can. I started in game design, and it shapes how I work: I look for the moment someone hesitates and ask what would make the next step feel obvious, maybe even fun.

I've worked in design for seven years, six of them in product design and UI/UX. I designed apps, websites, games and enterprise software for education, construction and tech, led a team of four designers on 13+ products and built design systems from zero. I also did product design and UI/UX for indie games, including their English and Spanish localization. Then I started coding what I design: today I work in Figma and React, and what I enjoy most is delivering the whole experience, from the first sketch to the screen in production.

From 2025 to 2026 I worked with licensed betting brands in Brazil. The stakeholders trusted my game design background, and I was the technical owner of the gamification of three brands, from research to code. Adoption was low, players dropped off on acquisition screens and retention was falling. I built the answer around rewards and consistency: clear messages for each audience, segmented CRM campaigns, A/B tests, new themed dynamics every month and screens that load fast. Along the way I built the brands' design systems and an Email Builder, worked daily with international front-end teams, and the 4Play redesign I led was presented as a reference in meetings in Europe, Cyprus and Latin America.
