04 · The rules4

Four rules, written down.

Round-ups are easy to explain and easy to get wrong. These are the only rules Vantle follows.

  1. 01

    Rounds up to the next dollar

    +$0.65

    $4.35 becomes $5.00. The $0.65 goes to your vaults.

  2. 02

    Exact dollars add nothing

    $0.00

    A $24.00 book stays $24.00. Nothing is set aside.

  3. 03

    Swept every Monday

    MON

    Change collects through the week and moves in one transfer.

  4. 04

    Boost if you want

    ×1

    ×2 or ×3 sets aside double or triple the change. Off unless you turn it on.

  5. $

    What it costs

    $3/MO

    $3 a month. No fee to pause, change the split or move money out.

    Example pricing for this concept.

    1. 01

      Brief I set myself: a launch page for a fictional fintech card in one HTML file, with no photos. Every visual is built in code with three.js, canvas and SVG.

    2. 02

      Onboarding: the page asks for one small commitment before the big one. You name a goal and see a date first, and the waitlist form then speaks to that goal.

    3. 03

      Conversion and trust: the rules, the off switches and the price sit before the form, every projection carries its assumptions next to it, and no interest rate appears anywhere.

    4. 04

      What I'd test next: whether showing the projected date in the hero, before any scrolling, raises demo completion and waitlist sign-ups.
